💡 Analyzing the Bottom Light Issue

Let's look at what happens when the bottom of your green screen doesn't get enough light, causing the key to fail.

🚨 The Problem: Underlit Green Screen

In the video above, you can see that the bottom section of the screen is not keyed out. Instead, it appears dark and noisy, blending with the subject. 🌑

Why this happens: The lights in your setup are likely positioned high up and aimed at your face/upper body, causing the light to fall off significantly by the time it reaches the bottom of the green screen. Because the bottom is too dark, OBS doesn't recognize it as "green" and fails to key it out.

✅ The Fix: Illuminate the Floor/Bottom Area

To get a clean key from head to toe, you need even lighting across the entire background. Here is how to fix it:

  • 💡 Add a floor light: Place a small fill light on the floor aimed upwards at the bottom half of the green screen to eliminate the shadow falloff.
  • 📐 Adjust main lights: If you don't have an extra light, try angling your main background lights down slightly or moving them back so their light spreads more evenly across the full height of the backdrop.
  • 🟩 Check your monitor: Look at your camera feed without the chroma key applied. The bottom of the screen should be roughly the same bright green as the top!

Once you balance the lighting, your chroma key will easily remove the entire background! 🚀

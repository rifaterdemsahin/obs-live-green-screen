💬 Implementing Captions

Captions make your content accessible and engaging. Here is how to add them to your live recordings and fast production workflow! 🎙️

🚀 Method 1: Live Captions in OBS (Zero Editing)

If you want to maintain the "fast production" zero-render workflow, you can burn captions directly into your video using OBS!

  • 🔌 LocalVocal Plugin: Install the OBS LocalVocal plugin. It uses AI to transcribe your speech locally and outputs a text source you can style directly in OBS.
  • 🌐 Window Capture Method: Open a browser tool like WebCaptioner. Add a "Window Capture" source in OBS to grab the text, apply a Chroma Key or Color Key to remove the background of the browser, and place the text over your video.
  • 🎨 Styling: Use a bold, readable font (like Montserrat or Impact) with a black outline or drop shadow so it pops against any background.

✂️ Method 2: Auto-Captions in Post-Production

If you don't mind a quick stop in an editor, this is the most popular way to get dynamic, animated captions for social media.

  • 📲 CapCut (Desktop or Mobile): Drop your OBS recording into CapCut. Click Text > Auto Captions. Apply an animated template, and export!
  • 🎬 Premiere Pro / DaVinci Resolve: Use the built-in speech-to-text tools to generate subtitles, style the track, and export.

Pro Tip: 🧠 Even if you use Method 2, your OBS setup saves you hours of green screen rendering, making the captioning step incredibly fast!

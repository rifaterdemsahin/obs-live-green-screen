🚨 Sanity Check: Layer Order!

In the screenshot above, look closely at the Sources panel:

  • Image (the background) is at the top of the list.
  • Capture Card Device (the camera) is below it.

⚠️ This is incorrect! OBS renders sources from bottom to top. If your background image is above your camera source, it will completely cover you!

✅ The Fix: Move the Background Down

To fix this and achieve a proper green screen effect:

  1. Click on the Image source in the Sources panel.
  2. Click the down arrow 🔽 (or drag it down) so it sits below the Capture Card Device.
  3. Make sure the Capture Card Device has the 🟢 Chroma Key filter applied so the background can show through!

Once the layers are ordered correctly, your setup is ready for fast production recording! 🚀
